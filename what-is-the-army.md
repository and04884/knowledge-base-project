# What Is the U.S. Army?

The U.S. Army is a branch of the Armed Forces responsible for land-based military operations. It combines discipline, teamwork, and service to protect national interests.

## Core Missions
- Defend the nation
- Support civil authorities
- Conduct humanitarian operations
- Train and prepare forces for deployment
