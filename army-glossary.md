# Army Glossary

A quick reference for common Army terms.

- **MOS** — Military Occupational Specialty  
- **PT** — Physical Training  
- **NCO** — Noncommissioned Officer  
- **Chain of Command** — Hierarchy of leadership  
- **Ruck** — March carrying a loaded pack
